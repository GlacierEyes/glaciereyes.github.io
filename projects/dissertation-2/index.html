Modern Coup d'État

Marketing as a Modern Coup d'État: Developing a Strategic Framework for Analysing Electoral Influence on TikTok.

This dissertation examines how political marketing and creator-led communication can shape electoral influence on TikTok, using Romania's 2024 presidential election as a case study. It explores how campaign coordination, influencer guidance and platform-native video can make political messages feel personal, informal and organic rather than like conventional advertising.

Through a qualitative analysis of 20 campaign-related TikTok videos, the research examines how ideas about the ideal president were constructed through qualities such as integrity, competence, patriotism and proven action. It also considers indirect candidate framing, dissatisfaction with existing politics, calls for change, audience participation and voting mobilisation. The findings are brought together in a strategic framework for analysing how coordinated political communication may develop into wider audience engagement and potential electoral influence on the platform.
